Spot-on, collar or tablet: the right reminder against fleas and ticks

MémoPatte is a free Android app that reminds you when each of your pets’ parasite control is due, whether it’s a spot-on, a collar or a tablet. The reminder arrives on your phone, even offline.

How MémoPatte reminds you

  1. Add the parasite control

    Pick the “Parasite control” type, the schedule given by your vet or on the box, and the dose: 1 spot-on, 1 collar, 1 tablet…

  2. Get the reminder

    Three days before, to check you still have some, then on the day. It arrives even without internet, on holiday or at home.

  3. Log it in one tap

    Tap “Done” in the notification. Given on another day? Enter the date in the app.

MémoPatte doesn’t replace your vet: it logs and reminds you of what you planned together, without suggesting a dose, a product or a date.

Frequently asked questions

A monthly spot-on and a collar: does it handle both?

Yes. Each parasite control has its own schedule, in days, weeks or months, and its own reminder. One pet can have several.

Does MémoPatte recommend a product?

No. Choosing the product and the schedule is up to your vet. MémoPatte logs and reminds you of what you planned.

What if the reminder arrives late?

Android may delay reminders to save battery. The Reminders help page explains what to change, and the “Exact reminders” option makes them go off right on time.
