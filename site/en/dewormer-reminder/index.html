Never forget your dog’s or cat’s dewormer again

MémoPatte is a free Android app that reminds you of each of your pets’ dewormers, on the schedule set with your vet. The reminder arrives on your phone, even offline, and “Done” logs it in their health record.

How MémoPatte reminds you

  1. Add the dewormer

    Its name, its schedule (every month, every 3 months…) and, if you like, the dose to give: 1 tablet, 1 spot-on, 2 ml…

  2. Get the reminder

    Three days before, to check you still have a dose, then on the day. The reminder is scheduled on your phone, so it arrives even without internet.

  3. Tap “Done”

    From the notification or in the app. The next date is worked out for you, and the history keeps every dose.

MémoPatte doesn’t replace your vet: it logs and reminds you of what you planned together, without suggesting a dose, a product or a date.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I deworm my dog or cat?

It depends on their age, lifestyle and the product: your vet sets it. MémoPatte follows whatever schedule you enter, and never suggests a dose, a product or a date.

What if I forget to log it?

The dewormer moves to the top of the list as “Overdue”. MémoPatte nudges you just once, three days later. You can log it later, even on another date.

Does it work for several pets?

Yes, with no limit. Each pet has its own care, and the home screen shows them all on one screen.
