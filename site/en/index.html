What a forgotten treatment can cost

  • The kennel turns your dog away

    The vaccine booster was due a month ago. Without up-to-date vaccines, a boarding kennel or pet sitter may refuse to take them.

  • Given, or not?

    The evening pill: nobody remembers whether it was given. Give it again, or risk missing it?

  • The treatment stops too early

    Meant to last three weeks, it’s forgotten after ten days, and nobody notices.

You don’t have to remember anymore: MémoPatte remembers for you.

How it works

The app in eight screens, with Milo’s and Luna’s health record.

Reminders that arrive, even offline

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they go off without internet. Tap “Done” in the notification and it’s logged in your health record. For a reminder right on time, turn on “Exact reminders”.

If Android delays your reminders

What matters today comes first

Overdue and today’s reminders come first on the home screen. A missed one stays visible, with a single nudge and no guilt: log it whenever you like, even on another date.

All your pets at a glance

Milo, Luna and the rest on one screen: what’s due, for whom, and when. Tap a name to see only that pet’s care, and tap it again to see everyone.

Try it: tap Milo or Luna on the screen.

Every dose, to the day

For a treatment given several times a day, each dose has its own “Done” button. Some doses weren’t logged? Mark the ones that were given in one go; the history keeps every dose.

A health record for each pet

Each pet’s page brings together their vaccines, active treatments and weight. Anything overdue stands out, with the number of reminders to do.

Their vaccines and every shot

Every shot stays logged, with the date of the next one. Back from the vet, tap “Done”, or “Done on another day” if it was another day.

Their weight, as a curve

A weigh-in now and then is enough: the curve shows how it changes, with the difference since the last weigh-in. In kilograms or pounds.

Truly free, and your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no limit on pets. Export the health record as a PDF for your vet or a boarding kennel, or all your data as JSON or CSV, for free.

What MémoPatte doesn’t do

  • Replace your vet

    It logs and reminds you of what you planned with your vet. It never suggests a dose, a product or a date.

  • Show ads

    No ads in the app, and your data is never sold.

  • Force you to sign up

    Everything works on your phone without an account. You’ll only need one for MémoPatte Plus.

  • Track other animals

    Dogs and cats only, for now.

Frequently asked questions

When is MémoPatte coming out?

Soon, on Google Play. The date isn’t set yet: it will be announced here as soon as it is.

What about iPhone?

Not for now. MémoPatte launches on Android first, and there’s no date for iPhone yet.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything that works on your phone is free, with no ads and no limit on pets, exports included. Only MémoPatte Plus, for the cloud, will be paid.

Do I need an account?

No. You’ll only need one for MémoPatte Plus.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Android’s backup may keep a copy in your Google account (without photos). MémoPatte’s cloud will only be used with MémoPatte Plus. Your data is never sold.

What happens if I change phones?

The safest way: export your health record, then import it on the new phone. Android’s own backup may also bring it back, but with no guarantee: it depends on the device. Once MémoPatte Plus is available, signing in will be enough to get your health record back.

Does it replace my vet?

No. MémoPatte logs and reminds you of what you planned with your vet. It never suggests a dose, a product or a date.

Who makes MémoPatte

MémoPatte was born from a forgotten dewormer. I’m Gaëlle, an independent developer in France, and I’m building the app I wish I’d had: simple, honest, and working even offline.

Three promises guide it: everything that works on your phone stays free, your data is never sold, and you can export it whenever you like.

No investors, no ads: the app is funded by MémoPatte Plus, for those who want cloud backup. If something’s off, write to me.
