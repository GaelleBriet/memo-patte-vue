MémoPatte privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Who’s responsible for your data?

MémoPatte is an Android app published by Gaëlle Briet, a sole trader (entrepreneure individuelle) based in France. She’s the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Her full details are in the legal notice (in French).

For any question about your data: memopatte@gaelle-briet.fr.

2. In short

  • MémoPatte works without an account. Your health records (pets, vaccines, treatments, weigh-ins, photos) and your reminders are stored on your phone only. We can’t access them.
  • If you choose MémoPatte Plus, a paid option, you create an account and your health record is backed up on servers in the European Union, so you can restore it and use it on several devices.
  • Usage statistics are only turned on if you accept them. You can change your mind at any time.
  • No ads, and your data is never sold.

5. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone: it’s under your control. It’s erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Plus account and backup: as long as your account exists. If your Plus access ends and isn’t renewed, your backup is kept for 12 months, then deleted. We email you a month before. When your account is deleted, everything is erased right away.
  • Purchase token: the length of your Plus access, plus 60 days.
  • Usage statistics: 12 months.
  • Messages with us: 1 year after your request is closed.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Export your data: in the app, Settings › My data › “Export my data”, as JSON or CSV, for free.
  • Delete your Plus account and all its data: in the app, Settings › Account › “Delete my account”, or by email. It’s all explained on the Delete your MémoPatte account page.
  • Turn off usage statistics: in the app, Settings › Privacy.
  • For anything else, email memopatte@gaelle-briet.fr. We reply within a month.
  • You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority in your country.

7. Refunds

  • Within 48 hours of your purchase, you can ask Google Play for a refund directly.
  • After that, email us at memopatte@gaelle-briet.fr. We refund you if the app has a proven malfunction.
  • You manage and cancel your subscription in Google Play. Once you’ve cancelled, your Plus access stays active until the end of the paid period.

8. Security

Everything exchanged with our servers is encrypted. Plus data is encrypted at rest by our host and kept separate: each account can only read and change its own data. Passwords are never stored in plain text. If a data breach puts you at high risk, we’ll tell you, as Article 34 of the GDPR requires.

9. Minors

MémoPatte is meant for people aged 18 and over. If you think a minor has created an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

10. Android permissions

  • Notifications: to show your vaccine and treatment reminders. Asked for when you have your first reminder, never on first launch.
  • Photos: MémoPatte uses the Android photo picker and only receives the photo you pick. It can’t access your gallery or your camera.
  • Storage, on Android 7 to 10 only: to save an export to your phone’s Documents folder. Asked for the first time you save an export. Never asked for on Android 11 and later.
  • Without asking you: internet and network status access (Plus account and backup, purchases, statistics if you accept them), rescheduling reminders after your phone restarts, and purchases through Google Play.

No location, no contacts, no text messages, no microphone.

11. Changes

This policy may change: the last updated date is at the top of the page. If there’s an important change, we’ll let you know. Every version is available in the site’s public history.
