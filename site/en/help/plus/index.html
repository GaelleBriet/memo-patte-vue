What does MémoPatte Plus add?

Everything you do on your phone is free, with no account: your pets, their vaccines, treatments and weigh-ins, reminders, and JSON, CSV and PDF exports. MémoPatte Plus is the subscription that will add a cloud backup of your health record, photos included. It’s coming soon.
