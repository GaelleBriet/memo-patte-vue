How do I get my health record onto a new phone?

  1. On the old phone, open Settings › Backup › “Export a copy”, and send the file somewhere the new phone can reach it (Google Drive, email…).
  2. On the new phone, install MémoPatte, then tap “Import a MémoPatte export” on the welcome screen and choose that file.

If Android’s backup was on, or if you transfer your apps from one phone to the other, Android may also put your health record back when it installs MémoPatte (see What does Android’s backup keep?). Either way, photos don’t come along; allow MémoPatte’s notifications again, and its exact reminders if you used them.
