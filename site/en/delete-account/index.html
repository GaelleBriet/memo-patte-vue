Delete your MémoPatte account

MémoPatte is an Android app by Gaëlle Briet. This page explains how to delete your MémoPatte Plus account and all the data that goes with it.

Option 1: in the app (recommended)

  1. Open MémoPatte, then Settings › Account › “Delete my account”.
  2. The screen shows what you’ll delete and what stays. Tap “Continue”.
  3. Confirm it’s you: enter your password, then tap “Confirm”, or, if you sign in with Google, tap “Sign in again with Google”.
  4. Confirm with “Delete”. The app deletes your account right away.
  5. If you like, then choose “Also erase this phone’s data”. This step asks for a separate confirmation.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. Email contact@memopatte.app from your account’s email address, with the subject “MémoPatte account deletion”.
  2. We reply to confirm the request really comes from you.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days of your request, and confirm it by email.
Email us to delete my account

What we delete

  • Your MémoPatte account and your email address
  • Your cloud backup (your pets and their health record)
  • Your backed-up photos
  • Your usage data linked to this account

We don’t keep any of it, except technical connection logs (IP address, sessions), which Supabase deletes on its own schedule. Our host’s backup copies then expire on the host’s own schedule.

What stays

  • Your full health record on your phone: it still works without an account. To erase it too, use the option the app offers after deletion, or uninstall MémoPatte.
  • Your purchase history with Google Play, which sells MémoPatte Plus as the merchant and keeps it under its own rules.

Delete only your cloud backup

You can keep your account and erase only your backed-up data and photos: email contact@memopatte.app from your account’s email address and say “delete only my cloud backup”.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.
