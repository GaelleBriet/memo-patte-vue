Your dog’s or cat’s vaccine record, on your phone

MémoPatte is a free Android app that keeps each of your pets’ vaccines, with the date of the shot and the next one. Two weeks before, it reminds you to book an appointment with your vet.

How MémoPatte helps

  1. Log the vaccine

    Its name (DHPPi, feline panleukopenia, rabies…), the date of the shot and the next date given by your vet.

  2. Book the appointment

    Two weeks before it’s due: “Remember to book a vet appointment.” Then a reminder on the day, at the time you choose.

  3. Keep the history

    Every shot stays in the vaccine’s page. Export the health record as a PDF for a boarding kennel, a pet sitter or a new vet.

MémoPatte doesn’t replace your vet: it logs and reminds you of what you planned together, without suggesting a dose, a product or a date.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace the official vaccination booklet?

No. The booklet or passport signed by your vet remains the official document, for travel for example. MémoPatte helps you never miss a date.

What about a puppy’s or kitten’s first vaccines?

You can plan a vaccine before the first shot, on the date set by your vet: MémoPatte reminds you of it the same way.

When does a vaccine reminder arrive?

Two weeks before it’s due, then on the day, at the time set in Settings › Reminders. The early reminder can be turned off there.

Other reminders

  • Dewormer reminderTablet, paste or spot-on, on the schedule set with your vet.
  • Parasite control reminderSpot-on, collar or tablet against fleas and ticks.
  • Multiple petsAll your dogs and cats on one screen, no limit.
