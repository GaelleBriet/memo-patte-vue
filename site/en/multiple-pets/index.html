All your dogs’ and cats’ care, on one screen

MémoPatte is a free Android app that brings together the vaccines, dewormers, parasite control and medication of all your pets in a single list, sorted by urgency. No limit on pets, no account.

What MémoPatte does for the whole household

  • One “To do” list

    Overdue first, then what’s due today, then what’s next. Each line says which care, for which pet, and when.

  • One pet at a time

    Tap a name at the top of the home screen to see only that pet’s care. Their health record keeps their vaccines, treatments and weight curve.

  • A PDF for whoever looks after them

    Before you leave, export the health record of all your pets, or just one, as a PDF for the person caring for them.

MémoPatte doesn’t replace your vet: it logs and reminds you of what you planned together, without suggesting a dose, a product or a date.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on pets?

No. The number of pets is unlimited, and it’s not a paid option.

Dogs and cats together?

Yes. MémoPatte tracks dogs and cats, not other animals yet.

What about weight?

Each pet has its own weight tracking, with its curve, in kilograms or pounds.

Other reminders

  • Dewormer reminderTablet, paste or spot-on, on the schedule set with your vet.
  • Parasite control reminderSpot-on, collar or tablet against fleas and ticks.
  • Vaccine recordEvery shot logged, and a reminder two weeks before it’s due.
